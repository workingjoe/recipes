**Parboiling with Absorption (PBA) Method - Step-by-Step**

Based on research from the University of Sheffield's Institute for Sustainable Food, here are the specific measurements and timing for this arsenic-reducing technique:

**Ingredients & Equipment**

- **1 cup** uncooked white rice (unwashed)
- **Pre-boiled water** (hot tap water or boiled then cooled slightly works too)
- Saucepan with tight-fitting lid
- Colander/strainer
- Measuring cup(s)

**Detailed Steps**

| **Phase** | **Measurements** | **Timing** | **Action** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1\. Initial Boil** | 1 cup rice + ~2 cups pre-boiled water (enough to cover grains by ~1 inch) | Bring to vigorous boil, then cook **5 minutes** | Do not cover; keep water bubbling vigorously |
| **2\. Drain & Rinse** | Drain completely | Brief rinse with hot water | Rinsing helps remove released arsenic from grain surface |
| **3\. Absorption** | 1 cup rice + 1.5 cups fresh pre-boiled water (**1:1.5 ratio**) | **10–12 minutes** on low heat | Cover pot, reduce to low simmer; do not lift lid |
| **4\. Rest** | None | **5 minutes** off heat | Keep covered, no steam escaping |
| **5\. Serve** | Fluff with fork | Immediate | Ready to eat |

**Key Details**

**Water temperatures matter:** Using pre-boiled water in both phases helps prevent additional contaminant introduction and maintains consistent cooking temperature.

**Why these ratios work:**

- The initial high-water phase (step 1) leaches out arsenic through the excess water volume
- The absorption phase (step 3) uses standard cooking ratios so nutrients aren't lost downstream
- The final resting period allows moisture redistribution without further cooking

**Expected Results**

According to the Sheffield study, this method removes **up to 74% of arsenic from white rice** (and ~50% from brown rice) while retaining significantly more nutrients than the simple "pasta method" where all water is drained away.

**IF** _your rice is too firm or too soft, adjust the absorption phase by 1–2 minutes as needed for your setup._
